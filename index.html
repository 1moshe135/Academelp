Type items by hand, or use the bookmarklet

One item per line. Everything after the title is optional and can come in any order — a date, a type, a link, done, or grade:88:

Assignment 12 | 2026-09-20
Final exam | 15/01/2027 | test | grade:88

Set the course above, or make it the first field of each line (Intro to CS | Assignment 12 | 2026-09-20). Lines starting with # change the defaults from there on:

#path Computer Science
#course Intro to CS
#type lesson
